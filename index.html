Varnan Sarangian

I'm a third-year PhD student in the Department of Statistics and Actuarial Science at the University of Waterloo, where I'm fortunate to be advised by Dr. Aukosh Jagannath and Dr. Shoja Chenouri. My work is mainly concerned with probability theory and its applications. My PhD is supported in part by an Ontario Graduate Scholarship (OGS).

Before my PhD, I completed a Bachelor of Computer Science at the University of Waterloo.

Research interests: dynamics of learning algorithms, median-of-means estimation, distributionally robust optimization
